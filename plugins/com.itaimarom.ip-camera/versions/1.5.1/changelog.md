## Camera tiles and controls

- Movement arrows on camera tiles, wall cells and the enlarged window, with a transparency setting.
- A **Press script** setting: run a Macro Deck script from the picture while keeping the arrows. Macro Deck gives a tile press either to its controls or to its Short Press flow, so a script is the way to run actions with the arrows on.
- The enlarged window now draws its arrows over the video instead of sharing the height with it.
- A camera reports its frame size before the first live frame, and remembers it for cameras configured earlier, so clients that reserve the aspect ratio do not stretch the picture.
- Store copy and screenshots refreshed for the camera wall, split and four-camera tiles.
