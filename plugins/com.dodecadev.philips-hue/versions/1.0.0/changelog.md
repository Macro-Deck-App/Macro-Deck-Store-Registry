First release for Macro Deck 3.

- **Set scene**, **Update light** and **Adjust light** actions, with bridge, light, scene and room pickers filled from your bridge
- Pair one or more Hue bridges with the bridge's link button; bridges are found automatically on your local network
- Bridges that get a new IP address are found again automatically
- Clear messages when a bridge can't be reached or no longer accepts Macro Deck, with a button to fix it
- Coming from Macro Deck 2? The migration wizard brings over your paired bridges and your Hue buttons
