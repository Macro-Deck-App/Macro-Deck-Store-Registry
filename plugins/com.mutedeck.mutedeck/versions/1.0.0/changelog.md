Control your meetings from [Macro Deck](https://macro-deck.app/) through [MuteDeck](https://mutedeck.com): mute and unmute, start and stop your camera, screen share and recording, leave the meeting, bring the call to the front and run MuteDeck custom actions such as raise hand or reactions. Buttons show your live meeting status, and the plugin keeps the variables of the Macro Deck 2 plugin so existing setups carry over.

MuteDeck works with Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet, Webex and many more.
